# ✨ Welcome to Fruicroc

The story of Fruicroc began quite simply… with my eating habits. I eat very little during the day, and my diet relies mostly on healthy snacks. For a long time, I used to buy crunchy fruits in Lebanon, but here, I couldn't find the taste and quality I loved.

So I decided to make them myself… at home, for me and my daughter ❤️

The road wasn't easy.
There were many trials, tiredness, and challenges. But I stayed determined until I reached the perfect result: crunchy fruits — natural, delicious, and made with love 🍓🥭🍎

Today, Fruicroc is not just a brand…
It's a story of passion, perseverance, and love for healthy snacks.

Thank you for being part of this adventure 🌸
**Jana .K**
